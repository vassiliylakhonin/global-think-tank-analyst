## Signal
The May 2026 Strait of Hormuz disruption is best read as a corridor, not a point forecast. The World Bank ([2026-05-07](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/strait-of-hormuz-disruption-sends-oil-prices-surging)) frames a baseline of **$86/bbl Brent in 2026 dropping to $70/bbl in 2027**, with an upside-risk band of **$95–115/bbl** (10–35% above baseline). Spot Brent on 2026-05-08 sat around $100–101/bbl ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/08/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-us-iran-fire-war-hormuz-ceasefire.html); [NewsX](https://www.newsx.com/business/oil-prices-today-may-8-2026-brent-crude-climbs-above-101-as-iran-us-tensions-fuel-hormuz-supply-fears-215289/)). Q2 2026 supply deficit projected at 3.7 mb/d (World Bank).

## Why it matters
- Procurement decisions anchored on today's spot misprice both directions of the corridor.
- For industrial energy buyers, the operationally binding risk is regional gas-spread volatility correlated with crude through LNG arbitrage, not Brent level alone.
- Ceasefire status is the dominant near-term driver; status oscillates daily.

## Decision question
For a European industrial energy buyer over the next 90 days, is the right structural move to commit to a price level, or to invest in optionality (shorter cover, pre-authorized hedge triggers, cap-and-floor renegotiation)?

## Quick assessment
- **Fact (from sources):** Brent ~$100/bbl on 2026-05-08; World Bank baseline $86, upside corridor $95–115; Q2 supply deficit 3.7 mb/d (World Bank, CNBC).
- **Assessment:** A range-aware hedge outperforms a directional hedge in oscillating markets like this one.
- **Assumption:** No regime-changing event inside 90 days.
- **Unknown:** Ceasefire durability; willingness of major exporters to use spare capacity.
- **Main risk:** Anchoring on today's print rather than the corridor.

## What to watch
- IEA monthly Oil Market Report supply/demand revisions.
- Hormuz tanker traffic and war-risk insurance premiums.
- TTF / NBP gas spread relative to Brent (decoupling under regional events).
- US/Iran negotiation-status official communications.

## What would change the judgment
- Durable ceasefire with traffic restoration → corridor collapses to baseline.
- A new named closure event >7 days → upside scenario becomes primary.
