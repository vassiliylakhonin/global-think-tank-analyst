## Signal
A live-source-backed memo on the May 1, 2026 OFAC "Operation Economic Fury" Iran shadow-banking action ([Treasury SB-0477](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0477)) was projected into an Agenda Intelligence MD JSON brief and scored. The structural-only score was **95/100**. With an honest evidence pack attached — which marked two analytical claims as `unsupported` and listed three `required_but_missing_sources` — the score dropped to **83/100**. The drop is the integration working as intended: dishonest packs would score higher and would violate this skill's evidence rules.

## Why it matters
- Tooling that *rewards* honest gaps (rather than penalizing them) is what makes a structural composition trustworthy.
- For compliance and risk teams piloting agent-assisted memo workflows, the lesson is to treat the score as a smoke test for *discipline*, not as a quality grade for *truth*.
- The only way the higher 95/100 is "earned" is if every claim is genuinely sourced; padding to keep the score up is an evidence-discipline failure.

## Decision question
For a team adopting GTTA + Agenda Intelligence MD, what target score is "good enough" *for that team's risk appetite*, and at what point does the team override a higher score because the unsupported assessments are themselves the load-bearing part of the memo?

## Quick assessment
- **Fact (from sources):** OFAC designated 35 entities and individuals on 2026-05-01 and explicitly named Hong Kong and the UAE as preferred shell-company jurisdictions for the Zarringhalam network ([Treasury SB-0477](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0477)).
- **Fact (from outputs):** The same memo+pack scored 95/100 without evidence pack, 83/100 with an honest evidence pack ([live demo doc](../../docs/integrations/agenda-intelligence-md-live-demo.md)).
- **Assessment:** Teams that anchor on the higher number will under-invest in source verification; teams that read the breakdown will catch where their analyst is reasoning beyond the evidence.
- **Assumption:** Agenda Intelligence MD's scoring rubric remains broadly stable in its weighting of unsupported_claims and required_but_missing_sources.
- **Unknown:** How many teams in production actually attach evidence packs vs. score brief-only.

## What to watch
- Updates to Agenda Intelligence MD's scoring weights (changes to evidence_support component would shift this conclusion).
- Adoption patterns of evidence packs in published examples vs. brief-only scoring.
- Whether MCP-mediated agent loops self-correct toward higher honest scores or toward higher dishonest scores under deadline pressure.

## What would change the judgment
- A scoring change that rewarded omission of unsupported claims (would invert the integration's value).
- Public evidence that production users routinely strip unsupported claims to game the score.
