## Signal
On **2026-05-07** the Council of the EU and the European Parliament reached a **provisional agreement** to simplify and streamline AI Act rules under **Omnibus VII** ([Council press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)). Headlines frame it as relief; the operative shape is more nuanced. National AI sandboxes are postponed to **2 August 2027**. The transparency-disclosure grace period for AI-generated content is **shortened from 6 months to 3 months**, with a new deadline of **2 December 2026**. A new prohibition is added on AI generation of non-consensual sexual and intimate content. Privileges for SMEs are extended to a new category of "small mid-cap enterprises" (SMCs).

## Why it matters
- Treating the package as a "haircut" misreads it: the synthetic-content transparency obligation is now closer, not further away.
- The GPAI supervision clarification narrows uncertainty about which authority asks the questions; it does not reduce substantive obligations.
- The new content prohibition will create downstream obligations for content-moderation, model-card disclosures, and vendor risk far beyond the headline.

## Decision question
For an AI provider with EU customer exposure, should compliance budget be cut (read: relief), held flat (read: monitor), or **redeployed** — pulled forward on synthetic-content transparency and pushed back on high-risk system work?

## Quick assessment
- **Fact (from sources):** Provisional agreement 2026-05-07; sandbox deadline → 2 August 2027; transparency grace period 6→3 months, deadline 2 December 2026 ([Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)).
- **Assessment:** Redeploy compliance budget rather than cut; the binding deadline tightening is the synthetic-content one.
- **Assumption:** Council and Parliament endorse without material amendment within ~90 days.
- **Unknown:** Final SMC threshold; treatment of dual-use content tooling under the new prohibition.
- **Main risk:** Cutting compliance investment now and then scrambling for the December 2026 transparency obligation.

## What to watch
- Formal adoption confirmations and Official Journal publication.
- Substantive amendments before formal adoption.
- Commission delegated/implementing acts on the new content prohibition.
- AI Office guidance specifying GPAI supervision carve-outs (law enforcement, border, judicial, financial).
- SMC threshold definition.

## What would change the judgment
- Material amendment narrowing or reversing the synthetic-content transparency relief.
- CJEU referral creating near-term legal uncertainty.
- Commission guidance interpreting the GPAI carve-outs more broadly than the press release suggests.
