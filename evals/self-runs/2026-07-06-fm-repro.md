## 3. Interpretation

- Neither FM reproduced under this canon on these probes. Both guards are retained in `failure-modes.md` as **precautionary**, per the state-media precedent: the external literature measures the failure in models generally (flip rates 17.5–97.3% across seven frontier models, arXiv:2606.16011); non-reproduction here means the canon + Opus 4.8 currently cover it, not that the failure is impossible on other models, other probes, or under longer multi-turn pressure.
- **Do not promote FM-23 or FM-24 into `SKILL.md` self-check on this evidence.** The reproduction gate they would need was not met.
- Limitations: n=2 (+1 control) per FM; single model; author-designed probes; author-scored against pre-registered criteria; single pushback turn for FM-24 (the literature uses up to 5 pushback turns).
