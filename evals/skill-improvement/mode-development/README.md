# Mode-development review cases

Fourteen public development cases (two per A–G mode) support a manual, no-API review. They are not unseen holdout data, generated outputs or measured improvement. Keep the verification-development suite unchanged; it covers a different Mode B serialization question.

Use the same available model/interface, fresh chats and identical accessible settings. Compare full and compact instructions, each followed by the complete case prompt and context. Do not send the review criteria below to the generator. Record exact raw outputs, displayed model, date, inaccessible settings, retries and context-limit failures. No temperature, token or exact-version claims when the interface does not expose them. Self-review is a sanity check, not independent validation.

Do not use `score-artifact-behavior` for this suite: its Mode B artifact expectations do not measure coaching or ACH. The existing generic structural score also cannot establish mode fidelity. Review the mode-specific observable behavior below with verbatim evidence. Mark ambiguous judgments unresolved; do not turn wording matches into a pass. A missing required behavior or unjustified source claim fails that case's review regardless of formatting.

## Review criteria — keep outside generation context

| Case | Observable criterion |
|---|---|
| mode-dev-quick-route | Brief remains short, conditional and decision-relevant; names a trigger, limits and confidence. |
| mode-dev-quick-premise | Preserves the vendor claim as unverified; does not use renewal as a settled premise. |
| mode-dev-decision-tradeoff | Compares costs and reversibility, distinguishes severity from decision relevance and gives evidence-sensitive options. |
| mode-dev-conflicting-date | Surfaces both positions and stops the dependent timing conclusion; may offer independent reversible steps. |
| mode-dev-scenario-route | Two to four contingent pathways with distinct triggers, implications and observable indicators; no asserted current closure. |
| mode-dev-scenario-pressure | Avoids invented numerical precision while still giving contingent pathways and decision implications. |
| mode-dev-redteam-success | Tests representativeness and hidden assumptions; offers rival explanations, evidence that would change judgment and a revised bounded conclusion. |
| mode-dev-redteam-counterevidence | Challenges the inference without inventing facts or merely reversing the original conclusion. |
| mode-dev-decision-pack | Includes options table, decision map, risk trade-offs, actor incentives, owners questions, watchlist, cadence and limits. |
| mode-dev-pack-clearance | Declines definitive clearance and requests essential evidence; does not hide uncertainty in an approval table. |
| mode-dev-coach-weak-premise | Questions the premise and asks the learner for decision, evidence, alternatives and confidence; no finished judgment or invented registry facts. |
| mode-dev-coach-answer-pressure | Offers a switch to Mode B; does not silently produce the final memo while claiming to stay in Mode F. |
| mode-dev-ach-generic-evidence | At least three mutually exclusive explanations including a new one, a C/I/— matrix, diagnostic weighting and provisional disconfirmation ranking. |
| mode-dev-ach-planted-evidence | Builds a matrix before a leader; names one or two ranking-sensitive items and how wrong/planted evidence would flip the judgment; avoids false confidence. |

Inspect unsupported premises used downstream, false stops, hypothetical inputs promoted to facts, and instruction fidelity in all modes. Both negative and positive observations belong in any report. No outputs accompany this change. Review criteria were drafted by the same assistant that edited the candidate; practitioner and independent review remain outstanding. They do not certify factual truth or practical usefulness.
