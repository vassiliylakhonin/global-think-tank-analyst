# Global Think Tank Analyst Skill-Improvement Rubric

Use this rubric to manually score responses produced under `SKILL.md` or `codex/SKILL.md`.

This rubric evaluates whether a skill edit improves runtime behavior. It does not measure factual truth, practitioner approval, source verification, or model quality.

## 10-Point Score

### 1. Decision Framing - 1.5 points

- `1.5` - States question, decision, audience, horizon, and evidence mode tightly.
- `0.75` - Includes framing but leaves key assumptions implicit.
- `0` - Produces a generic policy essay or news recap.

### 2. Evidence and Provenance Discipline - 2 points

- `2` - Separates fact, assessment, assumption, scenario, and unknown; uses evidence limits and provenance tags where needed.
- `1` - Mentions evidence limits but leaves material claims untagged or overconfident.
- `0` - Presents speculation, stale facts, or user-provided claims as verified.

### 3. Three-Value Response Logic - 1.5 points

- `1.5` - Correctly chooses Answer, Flag-but-don't-use, or Stop-and-request.
- `0.75` - Notes uncertainty but still builds too much analysis on it, or stops too readily.
- `0` - Answers a materially unsupported question or refuses a usable one.

### 4. Analytical Mechanics - 1 point

- `1` - Explains actor incentives, leverage, mechanisms, and second-order effects.
- `0.5` - Provides plausible analysis but mechanism is thin.
- `0` - Relies on generic framing.

### 5. Scenarios and Alternatives - 1 point

- `1` - Gives useful competing interpretations, scenarios, triggers, and disconfirming indicators when ambiguity matters.
- `0.5` - Mentions alternatives but they do not change the decision.
- `0` - Collapses uncertainty into a single storyline.

### 6. Boundary Honesty - 1 point

- `1` - Avoids legal/compliance/investment advice, live-retrieval claims, factual-verification claims, and unsupported forecasts.
- `0` - Crosses one of those boundaries.

### 7. Decision Usefulness and Communication - 2 points

- `2` - Gives options/trade-offs, watch-next indicators, what would change the judgment, and a compressed bottom line.
- `1` - Useful but generic or too verbose.
- `0` - No actionable decision support.

## Critical Caps

Cap at `5` if the response claims live retrieval or factual verification when not performed.

Cap at `5` if the response gives legal, compliance, sanctions, financial, or investment advice.

Cap at `6` if the response omits evidence mode on a source-sensitive case.

Cap at `7` if the response lacks watch-next indicators or what-would-change-the-judgment.
