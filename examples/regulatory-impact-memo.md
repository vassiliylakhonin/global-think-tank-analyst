## Executive takeaway

**Key judgment (Moderate confidence):** The binding constraint over 12 months is reporting capacity and embedded-emissions data quality, not the financial cost of certificates. The firms that lose volume first are those whose EU buyers cannot defend their own CBAM filings using supplier data.

## Facts vs assessments

- **Fact:** CBAM applies to specified product categories at EU import.
- **Fact:** Importers of record carry the reporting and certificate obligation.
- **Assessment:** EU buyers will increasingly select suppliers on auditable emissions data, not just price.
- **Assessment:** Firms that cannot supply credible product-level emissions data will see margin compression before they see lost contracts.
- **Assumption:** No major design change to CBAM scope in the 12-month horizon.

## Actor incentives

- **EU importers:** want defensible data to minimize their own certificate cost → reward suppliers that reduce their reporting burden.
- **Competing exporters:** that have already invested in MRV (measurement, reporting, verification) gain a non-price advantage.
- **The firm:** wants minimum disruption → temptation to under-invest in MRV until forced.

## Options and trade-offs

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Build product-level MRV now | Defends premium positioning | Capex and reporting overhead |
| Outsource emissions verification | Faster | Higher per-unit cost, less auditable |
| Accept default values | Cheapest now | Worst long-term margin outcome |

## Watch-next indicators

- Buyer requests for product-level emissions data (volume, specificity).
- Competitor MRV announcements in the same product category.
- Changes to EU default values for the relevant CN codes.
- Domestic carbon pricing or rebate signals from local authorities.

## Confidence and key unknowns

- Confidence: **Moderate** on direction; **Low** on magnitude of margin impact in any single quarter.
- Unknowns: buyer-by-buyer MRV requirements; pace of default-value tightening; competitor MRV maturity.

## What would change the judgment

- Material change in CBAM product scope.
- A major buyer publicly disqualifying suppliers without product-level MRV.
- Domestic carbon-pricing scheme that qualifies for CBAM offsetting.
