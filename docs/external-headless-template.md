# External Review Template

Use this template when asking a domain practitioner to review a case packet.

This is not a marketing form. The goal is to capture useful criticism, not to
collect praise. A review should not be published with attribution unless the
reviewer explicitly agrees.

## Reviewer Context

1. What type of work do you do?
2. Which part of this case is closest to your real workflow?
3. Which part is outside your expertise?

## Core Questions

### Decision Framing

1. Is the decision question clear?
2. Is the audience realistic?
3. Is the time horizon appropriate?
4. What would you change about the framing?

### Evidence Discipline

1. Are facts, assumptions, assessments, scenarios, and unknowns separated clearly?
2. Are evidence limits stated honestly?
3. Does the packet imply verification that did not happen?
4. Which sources would be mandatory before operational use?

### Domain Quality

1. Is the risk mechanism plausible?
2. Are any important actors, channels, or incentives missing?
3. Are any claims too strong for the evidence mode?
4. What would make the analysis misleading in a real workflow?

### Usefulness

1. Are role-based implications useful?
2. Are watch-next indicators concrete enough?
3. Does the output help decide what to do next?
4. Would you use this as a starting point, after revisions, or not at all?
